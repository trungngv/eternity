## Lessons

### Stop Being Right to Lead Better
> "you have to learn to quit being right all the time and quit being smart all the time and quit thinking this is a contest about how smart you are and how right you are and realize that i'm here to make a positive difference in the world"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LFhyL4pqqc8&t=0s)

Leaders must abandon the need to constantly prove their intelligence and correctness, shifting focus instead to making a positive difference in others' lives. This mindset change is fundamental to effective leadership and better relationships.

### Follow Up Consistently for Real Change
> "the key to making everything work is you have to follow up and stick with it...leaders that do talk to people and follow up to the degree they make it a systematic process...huge improvement"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LFhyL4pqqc8&t=420s)

Research on 86,000 leaders shows that follow-up and sustained effort are essential for lasting behavior change. Without consistent reinforcement, improvement reverts to random chance or disappears entirely.

### Perception Changes Through Sustained Behavior
> "four months ago i said i wanted to be a positive and open-minded listener based on last two months give me ideas for the next two months...when you do follow up...you think wait you've done a good job"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LFhyL4pqqc8&t=540s)

Others' perception of you only shifts when you demonstrate consistent new behavior over time. A single lapse after months of change can reinforce old beliefs, so continuous effort is required to build trust.

### Breathe to Think, Don't Just React
> "before i speak breathe and ask one question is it worth it...if you have to ask at work is my comment going to improve this other person's commitment if the answer is no breathe again"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LFhyL4pqqc8&t=900s)

Taking a breath before responding creates a crucial pause that shifts you from automatic reaction to conscious reasoning. This simple practice allows you to make better decisions about whether to speak at all.

### Stop Adding Value That Diminishes Ownership
> "well the quality of the idea may go up five percent my commitment to execute this idea just went down fifty percent it's no longer my idea boss now it's your idea"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LFhyL4pqqc8&t=840s)

When leaders improve others' ideas rather than letting people own them, commitment drops significantly even though idea quality may slightly improve. Allowing others to execute their own ideas builds engagement.

### Avoid Three Words That Kill Dialogue
> "just never start a sentence with three words no but or however if you talk to me and i say no first word no was it shot you're wrong...but what does butt mean disregard everything you said"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LFhyL4pqqc8&t=3600s)

Beginning sentences with 'no,' 'but,' or 'however' automatically discounts what others say and shuts down conversation. These three words are conversational killers that indicate defensiveness rather than genuine engagement.

### Make Peace With What You Cannot Control
> "every decision in life is made by the person has the power to make the decision make peace with that...if you can sell it you sell it if you can change it you change it if you cannot sell it and you cannot change it...let it go"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LFhyL4pqqc8&t=3960s)

Every decision is ultimately made by whoever has the power to make it. Rather than fighting this reality, accept it and focus your energy only on what you can actually influence or change.

### Leadership Is Serving Others, Not Being Right
> "never make the coaching process about yourself and your own ego and how smart you think you are make it about those great people you work with and how proud you are of them"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LFhyL4pqqc8&t=1200s)

The most effective leaders make their work about developing the people around them, not about demonstrating their own intelligence or proving they were right. Pride in your team matters more than proving your point.

### Track Daily Progress With Simple Scorecards
> "every day you fill out the little form at the end of the week you get a report card...the report card at the end of the week will not be as beautiful as the corporate values plaque you see stuck up on the wall"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LFhyL4pqqc8&t=4800s)

A daily scorecard answering key questions about goals, progress, meaning, happiness, relationships, and engagement creates awareness and accountability. Most people score disappointingly low on things that matter most.

### Success Is Alignment of Purpose, Goals, and Joy
> "i define success is as you go through life you have alignment between one your higher aspirations in life two what you're trying to achieve and three your enjoyment of the process of life itself regardless of the outcomes"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=LFhyL4pqqc8&t=5400s)

True success emerges from aligning three elements: your higher purpose and aspirations, your concrete achievements and ambitions, and your daily enjoyment of the process itself—not from outcomes alone.
