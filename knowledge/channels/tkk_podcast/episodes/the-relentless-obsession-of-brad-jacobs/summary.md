# The Relentless Obsession of Brad Jacobs
**Source:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=U-FlqVzbj0s)
**Date:** --

## Lessons

### Rigid business plans fail; adaptability wins
> "a lot of people have a rigid business plan that's spelled out for many years and that's it and it's very that doesn't usually work why because life changes markets change economies change"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=U-FlqVzbj0s&t=22s)

Markets and circumstances change constantly. Successful leaders abandon rigid long-term plans in favor of flexibility to capitalize on emerging opportunities. This agility separates high-performing companies from mediocre ones.

### Find broken systems and fix them
> "I looked at that org chart and said this is a messed up org chart which is great for making money if you can find something that's messed up and easy to unmess up boyah there's your money"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=U-FlqVzbj0s&t=24s)

Organizational dysfunction creates the biggest profit opportunities. By identifying companies with messy structures, redundant roles, and inefficiencies, you can systematically improve them and capture enormous value.

### Get the major trend right above all
> "you can get a lot of things wrong if you get the big Trend right...if you get the main Trend right you're gonna make a lot of money and conversely if you don't get the main Trend right you're swimming Upstream"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=U-FlqVzbj0s&t=7s)

You can execute nearly everything else imperfectly and still succeed if you correctly identify and capitalize on the biggest market trends. Conversely, perfect execution on the wrong trend leads to failure.

### Remain a perpetual student, embrace curiosity
> "I absolutely view myself as a student of life I'm I don't view myself as as a guru who's figured it all out by a long shot and I think if you keep that that element of profound curiosity"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=U-FlqVzbj0s&t=8s)

Successful leaders stay humble and view themselves as learners, not experts. This intellectual humility allows them to continuously absorb insights from diverse sources and spot trends others miss.

### Give undivided attention and listen without judgment
> "maybe the most maybe the single most powerful thing you can do in a relationship whether it's personal whether it's professional is to give someone your 100% like you're doing now you're giving me 100% of your attention"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=U-FlqVzbj0s&t=12s)

Complete, non-judgmental attention is one of the most powerful tools in leadership and relationships. It builds trust, encourages openness, and unlocks the best ideas from those around you.

### Therapy and self-awareness sharpen business judgment
> "I did a couple years of therapy for three hours a week for for a couple years so I I have spent a lot of time reflecting on how I think what my automatic thoughts are what my biases are what my cognitive distortions are"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=U-FlqVzbj0s&t=4s)

Understanding your own cognitive biases, emotional triggers, and automatic thought patterns through introspection directly improves your ability to think rationally and make better decisions under pressure.

### Tie executive compensation directly to shareholder returns
> "I love compensation plans for the senior Executives that have a big component of equity that's dependent on TSR total shareholder return so we look at what do the how does our stock perform versus called S&P 500"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=U-FlqVzbj0s&t=84s)

Aligned incentive structures ensure that employees only prosper when shareholders prosper. This creates a powerful win-win dynamic where management's financial success depends entirely on delivering shareholder value.

### Unscripted conversations reveal authentic truth
> "I don't want them to tell me ahead of time what they're gonna ask and I don't want them to tell the managers who they're interviewing to know what the questions are ahead of time I don't want our Executives or our Frontline employees to waste time"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=U-FlqVzbj0s&t=70s)

Prepared presentations and rehearsed speeches hide problems and mask reality. Real, spontaneous conversations with unexpected questions reveal the actual state of operations and organizational health.
