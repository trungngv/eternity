# The Psychology of Power | Michael Ovitz
**Source:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Wp1Wn6QkkKU)
**Date:** --

## Lessons

### Knowledge Is Power — Index It for Context
> "I have a thing I always say to people that work with me. Knowledge is power. And it works for you and against you. And it works for you if you embrace it, use it, read, and try to index it in your head for context."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Wp1Wn6QkkKU&t=60s)

Relentless reading and learning is the foundational edge shared by every top performer Ovitz observed. The key is not just consuming information but mentally indexing it so it can be retrieved as context when making real-time decisions.

### Admitting Ignorance Beats Fabricating an Answer
> "Don't lie if you don't have an answer. Here's your answer. Tell you what, Shane, I'm going to get back to you because I don't know the answer to that question, but I'm going to find out. That's highly acceptable."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Wp1Wn6QkkKU&t=180s)

The entertainment industry ran on people lying to appear knowledgeable, but saying 'I'll find out and get back to you' is more credible and more trusted than manufacturing false certainty. Intellectual honesty is a competitive differentiator.

### Self-Deception Is Where Everyone Goes Wrong
> "The first place everybody goes wrong is they start to believe things that aren't true. People tend to deceive themselves. I find that to be a common denominator amongst everybody across every kind of industry."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Wp1Wn6QkkKU&t=420s)

Believing your own press — good or bad — distorts judgment across every industry. The moment you start thinking you're superior to others, you lose the grounded self-awareness required to keep improving.

### Only Advocate for What You Genuinely Believe In
> "People were not allowed to recommend roles or material that they didn't believe in. That was a big downfall of the agency business... That's a real weak link in the chain. You had to have a passion for what you were doing."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Wp1Wn6QkkKU&t=540s)

Recommending projects or ideas you don't actually believe in to please others destroys your credibility and is the weakest link in any advisory chain. Authentic passion is not just ethical — it's strategically superior.

### Fighting Your Job Caps Your Performance at Mediocrity
> "If you're fighting going to work during the day, you're not going to perform to your capacity or potential."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Wp1Wn6QkkKU&t=1020s)

If you dread going to work, you cannot perform at your full capacity — and you will never stand out. Personal unhappiness at work isn't just a personal problem; it's a performance ceiling that makes excellence impossible.

### Stay Multi-Disciplinary to Connect With Anyone
> "You need to stay on top of multi-disciplinary careers, functions, and interests and hobbies so you have something to talk about."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Wp1Wn6QkkKU&t=1440s)

Ovitz deliberately built knowledge across cars, medicine, art, and science so he could find common ground with every client and partner. Broad curiosity isn't a distraction from your craft — it's the relationship infrastructure that accelerates your career.

### Power Is Ephemeral — Never Believe You Have It
> "There's no such thing as power. It doesn't exist... it's an ephemeral thing power and it's fleeting and it doesn't last. And if you don't believe that, take a look at anyone that's had it. It's like a lease. It has a closed end and never a good one."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Wp1Wn6QkkKU&t=1560s)

Industry power lists are nonsense because power is temporary by nature. Anchoring your identity to status or perceived influence makes you fragile when it inevitably fades — and it always fades.

### Never Badmouth Others — It Only Reveals Insecurity
> "If you don't have something nice to say, don't say it. And if you got a problem, take them in their office, shut the door, and let it out and fight it out and then shake hands, but don't drag it on."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Wp1Wn6QkkKU&t=1680s)

People who trash talk competitors or colleagues aren't projecting confidence — they're signaling insecurity. It makes listeners wonder what that person says about them, and it poisons organizational culture. Resolve conflicts directly and privately.

### Study Mistakes Obsessively — Not Successes
> "He didn't care about any of the good things I did or any of the things that turned out to be successful... Wouldn't know the mistakes so he wouldn't make them. That to me was not smart. It was wise."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Wp1Wn6QkkKU&t=3180s)

A young entrepreneur who met Ovitz asked only about his mistakes and what he'd have done differently — showing zero interest in his wins. Analyzing failures is where the actionable, transferable wisdom actually lives.

### Momentum Is the Most Underrated Force in Business
> "Momentum to me is the single most important thing in anything we do... In business, if you don't have momentum, you have no business."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Wp1Wn6QkkKU&t=4500s)

Momentum doesn't arrive instantly — it's built through deep expertise, relentless work, and competitive awareness. Once you have it, stopping even briefly is costly, because momentum compounds in ways that raw talent alone cannot.
