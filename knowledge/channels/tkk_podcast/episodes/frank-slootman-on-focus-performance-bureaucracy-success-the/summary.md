## Lessons

### Behavior is a choice, performance is a skill
> "behavior is a choice it's not a skill set"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=fv5Bozsj2xM&t=4s)

Behavior reflects deliberate choices and should be addressed immediately when misaligned with values, while performance issues deserve time to develop. This distinction is critical for building accountability and maintaining culture.

### Fast action on bad behavior defines your leadership
> "you want to get bad behavior you know out of an organization fast because that that is your leadership brand"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=fv5Bozsj2xM&t=7s)

Leaders must remove destructive behaviors quickly because tolerance signals weakness and invites more dysfunction. Your willingness to act decisively on misconduct is your most visible leadership signal.

### Business differs fundamentally from family
> "I don't think a business as a family unfortunately in a family you can't fire your family even though you sometimes want to"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=fv5Bozsj2xM&t=10s)

Unlike families, businesses must prioritize shared mission and performance over kinship. This reframe from family to professional sports team enables objective accountability and decisions.

### Not choosing compromises everything
> "we can't do everything so we have to choose and uh not choosing is the worst thing you can do because now you're compromising everything"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=fv5Bozsj2xM&t=13s)

Unlimited priorities are no priorities. Strategic success requires ruthless choices about what to do and what to abandon; attempting everything ensures nothing gets adequate resources.

### Influence matters more than hierarchy
> "hierarchy or church mean nothing to us you know we operate through influence"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=fv5Bozsj2xM&t=18s)

Organizational power flows through merit and persuasion rather than titles. This meritocratic approach maintains startup velocity and prevents bureaucratic bloat that larger companies inevitably accumulate.

### Get to the front lines for ground truth
> "there's no substitute you know for in inspecting things at the front lines"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=fv5Bozsj2xM&t=35s)

Second and third-hand information distorts reality. Leaders must directly observe customer interactions and marketplace conditions to avoid relying on filtered feedback and make sound decisions.

### References reveal what interviews conceal
> "The way to find out anything about people is to fully surround where that person has been"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=fv5Bozsj2xM&t=51s)

Interviews primarily test selling ability, not actual capability. Real insight comes from people who've worked directly with candidates about their track record, reputation, and actual impact.

### Fail fast and correct faster than competitors
> "you can do really well in the world by being a fast course corrector"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=fv5Bozsj2xM&t=48s)

Speed of course correction beats avoiding mistakes. Organizations that acknowledge errors quickly and adjust outpace those that defend bad decisions, creating competitive advantage through learning agility.

### Leadership sets culture through standards
> "leadership sets tone sets expectations and if you don't do that yeah you become the California DMV"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=fv5Bozsj2xM&t=54s)

Organizational culture emanates from what leaders tolerate and emphasize. High standards attract ambitious people and create energy; low standards breed mediocrity and inertia.

### AI will transform how we access data insights
> "we now have opportunities to to massively enrich and contextualize you know search"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=fv5Bozsj2xM&t=42s)

AI moves beyond keyword matching to contextual understanding, enabling organizations to ask harder business questions of proprietary data in near real-time. This creates unprecedented competitive advantage.
