# Adam Karr: Know Yourself, Pick Your Game, Build Your Blueprint (Lessons from a Janitor & Investing)
**Source:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=mQ-Z4_r3IzQ)
**Date:** --

## Lessons

### Know Yourself Before Choosing Your Game
> "how do we go about selecting the game that we're playing in life"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=mQ-Z4_r3IzQ&t=0s)

The foundation of success is understanding your own strengths, temperament, and natural obsessions. Selecting the right "game" to play based on this self-knowledge determines whether you can compete at the highest level.

### Set Your Life Up for Obsession
> "set your life up for your obsession cuz if you do that you're all in you're grinding at that in a way that very few other people will do"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=mQ-Z4_r3IzQ&t=4s)

Align your environment, schedule, and resources around what you're genuinely obsessed about. This creates the conditions for the kind of sustained effort that separates exceptional performers from everyone else.

### The Magic Lives in the Last Five Percent
> "the Magic's in the last 5%... really getting into that last five to understand it and build conviction about it was a Difference Maker"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=mQ-Z4_r3IzQ&t=27s)

Going deep enough to understand the last 5% of complexity in a business or domain is where breakthroughs happen. Surface-level analysis leads to surface-level results.

### Write Your Thesis and Measure Reality Against It
> "write it down show your work track it measuring it as that thesis is evolving and events occur measuring it relative to that"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=mQ-Z4_r3IzQ&t=18s)

Crystallize your thinking through writing and track how reality unfolds compared to your original thesis. This creates feedback loops that accelerate learning and improve decision-making.

### Pull on Threads That Disconfirm Your Narrative
> "anytime that comes up like you got to really focus on it like that's the most valuable information in the situation"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=mQ-Z4_r3IzQ&t=39s)

The most valuable information is what contradicts your current thinking. Actively seek out disconfirming evidence with the same intensity you'd seek out supporting evidence.

### Hold Convictions Loosely, Always Seek Opposing Views
> "he was very convicted about things... but it was always loosely held and so if he heard anything counter... he would really grab that and then totally flip his View"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=mQ-Z4_r3IzQ&t=40s)

Intellectual horsepower combines deep conviction with genuine openness to being wrong. The best decision-makers actively seek out people who disagree with them.

### Time Allocation Matters More Than Capital
> "you can get more Capital you can't get more time and so just you know as an investor... will doing that result in the highest return on time"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=mQ-Z4_r3IzQ&t=51s)

You can always raise more capital, but time is finite. Being rigorous about where you spend your time is more important than optimizing your capital deployment.

### Position Yourself So Adversity Offers Options
> "you can't live a 100 years and not go through uh all of the things that life has to offer from heartbreak to losing money in an investment... and like how you respond in your approach to those things"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=mQ-Z4_r3IzQ&t=58s)

How you respond to adversity matters as much as avoiding it. Resilience comes from positioning yourself financially and mentally so you have choices even in difficult circumstances.
