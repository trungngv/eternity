# Brad Jacobs: How Therapy Made Me Billions
**Source:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=pduZ-bfcKAQ)
**Date:** --

## Lessons

### Find Opportunity in Organizational Disorder
> "This is a messed up org chart. Which is great for making money. If you can find something that's messed up and easy to unmess, booyah, there's your money."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=pduZ-bfcKAQ&t=0s)

Identifying organizational inefficiencies and structural problems is where real wealth creation happens. When you can spot something broken and fix it systematically, you've found your path to substantial profits.

### Therapy and Self-Awareness Transform Business Performance
> "I did a couple years of therapy for three hours a week. So I have spent a lot of time reflecting on how I think, what my automatic thoughts are, what my biases are, what my cognitive distortions are, and I'm aware of those and I apply various techniques and tools."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=pduZ-bfcKAQ&t=5s)

Understanding your cognitive biases, automatic thoughts, and distortions through psychological work directly improves decision-making and leadership effectiveness. This mental training is as important as any business skill.

### Build Psychological Safety Before Difficult Conversations
> "You need to create an atmosphere. You need to create an environment that's a safe place. That's a zone where you're it's okay to be vulnerable. It's okay to say what you really feel."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=pduZ-bfcKAQ&t=13s)

Trust and openness require creating an environment where people feel safe to be vulnerable and authentic. This precedes any productive dialogue or change effort.

### Undivided Attention is Your Most Valuable Currency
> "The most single most powerful thing you can do in a relationship, whether it's personal, whether it's professional, is to give someone your 100% of your attention. I can see it. You're looking at me. You're listening to me."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=pduZ-bfcKAQ&t=12s)

Giving someone your complete non-judgmental attention is profoundly nourishing and transforms relationships—personally and professionally. It's both rare and extraordinarily powerful.

### Stay a Student, Not a Know-It-All
> "I like to be a student more than a teacher. I find a lot of people make the mistake as they get older or they get more successful, they think they know everything and they start teaching all the time."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=pduZ-bfcKAQ&t=8s)

Curiosity and humility improve with age if you maintain them deliberately. Many successful people make the mistake of assuming expertise and stopping learning, causing them to miss trends.

### Improvisation Beats Rigid Long-Term Plans
> "A lot of people have a rigid business plan that's spelled out for many years and that's it. And it's very that doesn't usually work. Why? Because life changes, markets change, economies change."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=pduZ-bfcKAQ&t=24s)

Markets, competitors, and opportunities change constantly. Rigid adherence to a multi-year plan causes you to miss windfalls and adapt poorly. Flexibility and the ability to capitalize on surprises create wealth.

### Simplicity Reveals Problems Complexity Hides
> "Bad ideas can easily hide in complexity but they can't hide in simplicity. It's hard to hide."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=pduZ-bfcKAQ&t=30s)

Bad business ideas, inefficiencies, and organizational dysfunction flourish in complexity. Simple structures, clear metrics, and straightforward processes expose reality and make improvement obvious.

### Align Executive Compensation with Shareholder Returns
> "I love compensation plans for the senior executives that have a big component of equity that's tied that's dependent on TSR, total shareholder return. I want their interest aligned with the shareholders."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=pduZ-bfcKAQ&t=27s)

Tying executive pay to total shareholder return relative to the S&P 500 ensures leaders make decisions that create real wealth, not artificial metrics. It's the ultimate alignment tool.
