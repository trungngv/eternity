# Greg Brockman: AI Is About to Go Parabolic! Here's What's Next
**Source:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6JoUcQ1qmAc)
**Date:** --

## Lessons

### Choose a Mission Worth Your Entire Life
> "If you could actually make a difference in how AI will play out in the world, like that would be a life well lived."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6JoUcQ1qmAc&t=0s)

Greg left Stripe not because it was failing, but because it wasn't his problem to solve. Choosing work you'd pursue even if it succeeded without you is the foundation of meaningful impact.

### Massive Compute Beats Clever but Limited Algorithms
> "That actually massive compute with simple algorithms. Right? That that is something where it doesn't just work in theory. It works in practice."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6JoUcQ1qmAc&t=540s)

OpenAI's DOTA experiment revealed that scaling simple, known-flawed algorithms with massive compute outperformed the best humans — a finding that became the blueprint for all future AI progress.

### True Prediction Requires Genuine Intelligence
> "If you really can predict the next word out of Einstein's mouth, you are at least as smart as Einstein."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6JoUcQ1qmAc&t=600s)

The ability to predict in novel, never-before-seen situations is not a pedestrian task — it is deeply connected to reasoning itself, which is why next-token prediction unlocked machine intelligence.

### High Stakes Missions Amplify Every Conflict
> "If you truly believe in the mission, if you truly believe in the possibility of creating machines that have the intelligence level of humans, it means the stakes always feel very high."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6JoUcQ1qmAc&t=720s)

When a team believes they are building transformative technology, even routine disagreements over credit or process take on existential weight — leaders must anticipate this dynamic.

### Keep Going When the Mission Truly Matters
> "I've just learned to just keep going for something that's worth it. Right, if you have a mission that matters, then you keep going through the ups and the downs."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6JoUcQ1qmAc&t=1500s)

Through firings, resignations, and personal doubt, Greg's core lesson was that a worthy mission demands you not let the inevitable high and low moments pull you permanently off course.

### Make Hard Decisions Without Delay
> "Make the hard decision. Have the hard conversation."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6JoUcQ1qmAc&t=1860s)

The lesson Greg had to learn more than once: the biggest organizational regrets come from knowing something was wrong — the wrong person, the wrong direction — and waiting too long to act.

### Encounter Reality as It Is, Not as You Wish
> "The core of OpenAI is really encountering reality as it is."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6JoUcQ1qmAc&t=2640s)

OpenAI's competitive advantage wasn't just compute — it was a culture of honestly confronting hard truths rather than doing Silicon Valley-style 'reality distortion,' which doesn't work in AI.

### Deploy Iteratively to Learn from Reality
> "What about if you take an approach where this is your hundredth system. You've had to solve this problem 99 times before with systems of increasing power."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6JoUcQ1qmAc&t=3300s)

Instead of building in secret and deploying once, iterative deployment means each release is your 'hundredth system' — your team is experienced, your safeguards are tested, and society has had time to adapt.

### Safety Is a Core Product Feature, Not a Constraint
> "Safety is actually a core product feature. Like no one wants a model that is not aligned with them."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6JoUcQ1qmAc&t=3480s)

A model that isn't aligned with the user is simply a bad product. Safety and alignment are competitive advantages, not sacrifices — because no one wants an AI they cannot trust.

### AI's True Promise Is Human Empowerment and Agency
> "My view of AI is it is about empowerment. It is about human agency."
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=6JoUcQ1qmAc&t=3960s)

The right frame for AI is not replacement but amplification: lowering the barrier to building, creating, and achieving goals so that human agency expands rather than contracts.
