# Dr. Julie Gurner: How to Reach Your Potential

## Lessons

### Motivation as Internal Pull, Not External Push
> "motivation is a pull it is something that like is internal that fuels you that drives you and a number of the people that I tend to work with are very obsessed"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=C5D6HAskUaE&t=60s)

Motivation is an internal force that fuels high performers, while discipline requires constant pushing. True excellence comes from being pulled by obsession rather than pushed by willpower, making motivation the more sustainable and powerful force for peak performance.

### Internal Drive Cannot Be Taught or Created
> "internal drive is something you can't teach uh and I'm very you know perhaps that goes against current wisdom I do think that as long as someone has that internal hunger and that internal drive"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=C5D6HAskUaE&t=240s)

You cannot instill hunger or drive in someone who doesn't possess it naturally. However, you can align existing internal drivers with opportunities and compelling vision to unlock motivation in those who have the foundational hunger.

### Imaginary Rules Limit Your Perceived Potential
> "we are also simultaneously ingesting other rules that are unspoken and that are really unsubstantiated right like what is possible for us um you know what are the things that we can do what are we capable of"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=C5D6HAskUaE&t=1260s)

We unconsciously absorb unspoken, unsubstantiated 'rules' about what's possible for us from our environment and upbringing. These invisible beliefs constrain our potential because we never question them, preventing us from seeing opportunities that actually exist beyond our perceived limits.

### Choose Survivor Mindset Over Victim Mentality
> "you can be a victim or you can be a Survivor and those are two very different cognitive positions you know you can't control what happens to you in either circumstance but one is very powerful"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=C5D6HAskUaE&t=720s)

When facing adversity, you can cognitively choose to see yourself as a victim or survivor—both face the same circumstances, but this mental choice determines your future. While you cannot control what happens, you control how you interpret and respond to it.

### Boundaries Create Freedom and Peak Performance
> "boundaries do free us and they allow us to live our best life but we have to really enforce them and we have to be really clear on what they are"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=C5D6HAskUaE&t=1740s)

Setting clear boundaries around time, commitments, and energy allocation actually liberates you to operate at your highest capacity. Boundaries enable focus on your areas of highest and best use, making them essential for both productivity and well-being.

### Build Resilience With Multiple Life Foundations
> "if you have only one leg of a stool it's easy to kick out right so like if you say hey everything that I am is my business it's when your business does poorly or it collapses it just shakes you"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=C5D6HAskUaE&t=1800s)

Like a single-legged stool that's easy to topple, putting your entire identity in one area (like work) is fragile. Multiple stable foundations—relationships, health, spirituality, career—create resilience so that struggles in one area don't collapse your entire life.

### Small Wins Build Compounding Confidence
> "if you can do small things challenge yourself in some small way where you're going to take a risk and go outside of yourself it translates and when you start having some wins you will start to pull that mindset in your work"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=C5D6HAskUaE&t=2940s)

Taking calculated risks in low-stakes environments builds swagger and confidence that transfers to bigger challenges. Each small victory proves to yourself that you can execute, making the next risk easier and creating a positive momentum spiral.

### Keep Goals Private, Share Only Results
> "when you share your goals and people give you praise it actually pulls from motivation to achieve them so it's why vision boards aren't really all that great for you"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=C5D6HAskUaE&t=3000s)

Publicly announcing goals actually reduces motivation because you receive a dopamine reward from praise before doing the work. Keeping goals private while sharing only results preserves motivation and allows you to build credibility through demonstrated achievement.
