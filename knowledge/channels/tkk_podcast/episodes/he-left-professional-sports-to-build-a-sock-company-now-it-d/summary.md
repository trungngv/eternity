## Lessons

### Longevity Beats Quick Wins in Any Career
> "careers are built on longevity and that really comes down to a mindset"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Irbt1eM5Olk&t=2s)

Sustainable success is built over years through resilience and persistence, not through shortcuts or hacks. This fundamental mindset applies equally to sports and business.

### Learn More From Setbacks Than From Winning
> "you can learn from winning but a lot of the most important lessons and the stories I tell and what I reflect on in my life has come from all the losses or the setbacks"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Irbt1eM5Olk&t=6s)

Victories feel good but provide limited insight. Losses and setbacks force deeper reflection and build the valuable lessons that compound over time.

### Everything Is Earned, Never Owed
> "it taught me that everything is earned not owed you had to do the work there was no substitute for the work"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Irbt1eM5Olk&t=10s)

There is no substitute for doing the actual work. Talent, luck, and connections don't replace effort—only work creates real achievement.

### Preparation and Positioning Trump Raw Talent
> "how can I be more prepared than any other athlete when it comes to the start line how can I study the course how can I take what I know"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Irbt1eM5Olk&t=71s)

Superior preparation and strategic positioning matter more than natural ability. Success comes from studying your competition, mastering overlooked details, and arriving more ready than everyone else.

### The Glamorous Outside Masks Messy Reality
> "from the outside looking in sports looks really sexy business looks really sexy but from the inside it's a bit of a mess it's a total mess in both"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Irbt1eM5Olk&t=13s)

Observers see only the shiny wins, not the early mornings, injuries, failures, and mental struggles. Understanding this gap prevents chasing false narratives of overnight success.

### Subtract to Grow, Don't Just Add More
> "I think to get more you should be doing less typically you should be subtracting"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Irbt1eM5Olk&t=69s)

The instinct is to add more people, products, and initiatives to drive growth. Real leverage comes from removing inefficiencies and doing less, better.

### Focus on Process and Control, Not Outcomes
> "The score will take care of itself instead of focusing on the revenue the score just focus on the things you control"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Irbt1eM5Olk&t=81s)

You cannot directly control results, only your inputs and effort. The most reliable path is to focus on doing the right things consistently; outcomes follow naturally.

### Authenticity Unlocks Freedom and Fulfillment
> "I wasn't living authentically you know and I was kind of limited by the belief of others"
[Watch this moment →](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Irbt1eM5Olk&t=86s)

Moderating yourself based on others' opinions is exhausting and limits potential. Real power comes from being authentically yourself, even when it risks disapproval.
