# Topic: Audience Building

Lessons about building and engaging an audience — before, during, and after content creation.

---

### Involve Your Audience During Production, Not Just at Launch
Most authors parachute their book on an unprepared audience at launch day. Sharing surveys, title polls, cover concepts, and behind-the-scenes struggles during writing builds co-ownership and a pre-warmed launch audience. Start with a simple survey: "What two questions on this topic would make the book worthwhile for you?"
