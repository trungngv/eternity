# Topic: Book Publishing

Lessons about the business and strategy of publishing a book — titles, marketing, distribution, and what makes books spread.

---

### The Title Is the Most Important Marketing Decision
Almost all books that sell more than 10,000 copies are driven by word of mouth, which means the title must survive being spoken aloud. Test titles rigorously — Tim Ferriss tested 30–50 options via Google Ads before landing on The 4-Hour Work Week. A great title evokes curiosity, FOMO, or emotional reaction and is fun and easy to say.

*Source: [The Book Whisperer: How to Write a New York Times Bestseller](https://youtube.com/watch?v=BaZwtB2GI8U) (2026-06-10)*

### Books That Sell a Million Copies Produce Visible Transformation
Viral word-of-mouth is earned when readers visibly change in ways that prompt questions from people around them. Books that create observable transformations — physical, behavioral, or intellectual — generate natural conversation that no marketing budget can replicate.
